3D Scene Graph Generation for Autonomous Robots using Hydra + Perception Encoder

Oakland University · Spatial AI & Robotics Lab · 2026–present

Building on the Hydra scene graph pipeline, this work replaces CLIP with Meta's Perception Encoder to generate richer semantic embeddings for place nodes in 3D spatial graphs. The goal is to enable robots to reason about environments at multiple semantic levels — room, object, and surface — for long-horizon planning and human-intelligible scene description. Deployed in a Docker-based pipeline on Ubuntu 24.04 with RTX 3080.

Multimodal HRI Data Collection with Pepper Robot and Intel RealSense D435

WPI RoboCareLab · Prof. Zachary Zhao · 2025–2026

Designed and deployed a multimodal HRI data collection system on the Pepper social robot, integrating Intel RealSense D435 depth sensing, tablet-based interaction interfaces, and audio pipelines. Ran live user experiments, and debugged the bridge server and sensor stack. Data feeds downstream HRI models for healthcare robot interaction design.

Safety-Critical Control with CBF-MPC-Transformer for Healthcare Robots

WPI · 8th International Conference on Industry of the Future & Smart Manufacturing · 2024

A unified framework combining Control Barrier Functions, Model Predictive Control, and a Transformer-based human-intent predictor to enforce hard safety constraints during human-robot collaboration. Validated on hospital AMR scenarios across surgical, stroke rehabilitation, and ICU telepresence settings with formal forward-invariance proofs.
